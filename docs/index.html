What it handles

ماذا يقدّم

Inbound calls

Answers enquiries, takes reservations, checks order status, logs complaints and opens service requests, around the clock.

Outbound calls

Booking confirmations, appointment reminders, follow-ups and feedback surveys, in each customer's own language.

WhatsApp and web chat

The same agent on chat, including Arabizi and Roman Urdu, with the conversation kept in one history.

Handoff to your team

Passes the conversation to a human agent with a transcript and a short summary, so the customer never repeats themselves.

Your systems

Connects to booking, CRM and ticketing tools to complete tasks, not just answer questions.

Your tone

Choose whether it mirrors the customer's mix, keeps to formal Arabic, uses Khaleeji only, or uses English only.

Built for hospitality and restaurants, banking and fintech, telecom, government services, real estate, healthcare, retail and e-commerce.

Made for the Gulf

صُنع للخليج

Data stays in-country

Hosted in UAE and Saudi cloud regions, so customer data doesn't leave the country.

Designed for local regulation

Built with UAE PDPL, Saudi PDPL (SDAIA) and banking regulators such as CBUAE and SAMA in mind.

Outbound calling rules

Outbound campaigns follow TDRA rules in the UAE and CST rules in Saudi Arabia.
